01 — The studio

Light, set and styling,
all under one roof.

Clone Pictures is a Lagos photo studio built around one idea: you should walk out with images that feel like you on your best day. We build warm, editorial sets, from sculpted arches and plush sofas to deep painted backdrops, and light every frame by hand.

Need a glow-up first? Our makeup and hair team can get you camera-ready before you step on set, at our studios in Yaba and Surulere.
